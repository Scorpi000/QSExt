# 参考文献与信息来源

## 一、学术论文

### 综述类

1. **Non-stationarity in Financial Time Series: A Taxonomy-Based Survey of Drift Detection, Adaptation, and Evaluation**
   - 来源：ScienceDirect / Neurocomputing, 2026
   - 链接：https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S092523122602045X
   - 要点：金融时间序列非平稳性的统一分类法；漂移检测方法全景（CUSUM/ADWIN/BOCPD/MMD）；适应策略与评估协议

2. **Handling Out-of-Distribution Data: A Survey**
   - 作者：Tamang et al.
   - 来源：IEEE TKDE, 2025
   - 链接：https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2507.21160v1
   - 要点：协变量偏移 vs 概念漂移的数学定义；KS/JSD/MMD/Hellinger 等方法比较

3. **Concept Drift in Unsupervised Data Streams**
   - 作者：Hinder et al.
   - 来源：Frontiers in AI, 2024
   - 链接：https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/artificial-intelligence/articles/10.3389/frai.2024.1330257/xml
   - 要点：无监督漂移检测的四阶段流水线（窗口→描述→不相似度→归一化）

### 因子衰减与拥挤

4. **Not All Factors Crowd Equally: Modeling, Measuring, and Trading on Alpha Decay**
   - 作者：Lee
   - 来源：arXiv, 2025
   - 链接：https://arxiv.deeppaper.ai/papers/2512.11913v2
   - 要点：双曲衰减模型 α(t) = K/(1+λt)；动量因子 R²=0.65；拥挤逆转 crash 概率 1.7-1.8×；2015 年后加速衰减

5. **Stochastic Optimization Under Distributional Drift**
   - 作者：Cutler et al.
   - 来源：JMLR, 2023
   - 链接：https://mlanthology.org/jmlr/2023/cutler2023jmlr-stochastic/
   - 要点：分布漂移下优化算法的理论收敛保证

### 市场状态与回撤

6. **Detecting Multivariate Market Regimes Via Clustering Algorithms**
   - 作者：Mc Greevy et al. (Kaiju Capital / Imperial College / Alan Turing Institute)
   - 来源：SSRN, March 2024
   - 链接：https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4758243
   - 要点：Wasserstein + MMD 的非参数无监督状态检测；配对交易和组合优化实证

7. **Measuring Strategy-Decay Risk: Minimum Regime Performance and the Durability of Systematic Investing**
   - 来源：Journal of Portfolio Management
   - GitHub：https://github.com/nolanalexander/minimum-regime-performance
   - 要点：MRP 框架——最优分割点使最差状态夏普最大化

8. **SHIFT: A Hierarchical Framework for Diagnosing Heterogeneous Performance Drift**
   - 作者：Singh et al.
   - 来源：arXiv, 2025
   - 链接：https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2506.00756
   - 要点：子群级别漂移诊断；McMD 统计量；定位到具体特征子集

### 因子生命周期

9. **Rebalance is not control: Understanding exposure persistence in factor indices**
   - 来源：FTSE Russell Research
   - 链接：https://www.lseg.com/en/ftse-russell/research/rebalance-is-not-control-understanding-exposure-persistence-in-factor-indices
   - 要点：自相关衰减率评估因子暴露稳定性；市场漂移 vs 信号演变

### 业界实践参考

10. **Drift detection in ADWIN using the Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) test**
    - 来源：Taylor & Francis
    - 要点：KS 检验在流数据中的漂移检测，p < 0.05 触发漂移信号

11. **The Gold Standard of MLOps: The Mathematics of Model Observability (Part 3)**
    - 来源：Google Cloud / Google Developer Forums
    - 链接：https://discuss.google.dev/t/the-gold-standard-of-mlops-the-mathematics-of-model-observability-part-3/366718
    - 要点：JSD 散度作为自动监控的黄金标准；KS 检验的计算成本分析
